PERSONA TWIN  (Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit)
=======================================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you own, for example  Documents\PersonaTwin
   (not inside "Program Files" and not inside a zip preview).

2. Double-click  PersonaTwin.exe
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Tab "1. Set up"  ->  press "Set up now" (needs the internet ONCE, roughly 3-6 GB).
   Tick the voice-cloning box only if you want replies in the person's voice.
   If asked, install Ollama (click through its installer). After setup, no internet is needed.

4. Tab "2. Build a twin"  ->  choose the folder where their photos, videos, chats and documents
   are stored (any folder on this computer). Pick which chat name is the person, choose one clear
   face photo, tick the permission box, press "Build the twin".

5. Tab "3. Talk"  ->  press Start. Pick a window, a fullscreen projector, or the hologram pyramid.
   Type in the box, or tick "Talk with the microphone".

Everything the app creates lives in the folders next to PersonaTwin.exe:
   runtime\   the private Python and packages
   data\      the twins: memories, voice clips, photos (personal data: keep it safe)
   app\       program files
To remove everything, delete the whole folder.

Tips
 * Only choose a folder that holds THIS person's material. Recordings where other people speak will
   blend their words into the twin.
 * A laptop without a graphics card works, but replies take a few seconds. The person's cloned
   voice is the slowest part; choose "Generic voice (fast)" on the Talk tab for quicker answers.
 * Use this only for people who agreed, or as family with the right to do so.
